CV02 Split Max

A larger clock. More room for your data.

CV02 Split Max separates the screen into two clear areas: oversized time on the left and a vertical information column on the right. It suits anyone who wants a prominent clock together with several useful data fields.

Key features

  • Extra-large time in a distinctive split layout
  • 5 information fields on the right, 4 editable
  • Date and battery level
  • Color options for time, date and battery value
  • Always-On Display
  • High contrast

Four fields you can change

Customize the four lower positions in the information column with compatible data providers. Options can include steps, calendar events, weather, sunset time or activity. The five visible information fields do not mean five editable complications.

A layout with a clear division

Hours and minutes stack on the left while information occupies individual rows on the right. Choose the clock, date and battery percentage color to match your preferred look.

Always-On Display

When the watch enters Always-On Display, the face uses a simplified view that keeps the time readable. Enable AOD in the watch settings; its availability and behavior also depend on your device and system settings.

How to customize

  1. Set CV02 Split Max as your active watch face.
  2. Touch and hold the face, then select Customize.
  3. Customize the four lower information fields and select a color for the clock, date and battery percentage.
  4. Confirm your selection, return to the watch face and check your data.

Option names may vary by watch and Wear OS version.

Extra data: Phone Battery Complication

The watch face works without Phone Battery Complication. This external app by amoledwatchfaces is required for selected additional data sources, such as phone battery. Install and configure it on both your Android phone and Wear OS watch. Available options depend on whether the selected field supports the data source.

Compatibility and complications

This watch face is designed for compatible Wear OS watches. Check device compatibility on Google Play before installing. Complication availability, appearance, text length and refresh behavior depend on your watch, Wear OS version and data provider. Not every complication works in every field. If a field remains empty, check the selected app’s permissions or choose another compatible source.

Questions about CV02 Split Max

Are all five information fields editable?

No. The four lower fields in the information column are editable.

What can replace the default data?

Choose a source supported by that field, such as calendar, weather, steps or activity. Options depend on your watch and installed apps.

Why is a complication name shortened?

Text length and presentation depend on Wear OS and the complication provider.
