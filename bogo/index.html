BOGO: how it works

Does a purchase made before this promotion qualify?

Yes. Earlier paid purchases of CV06 Side and CV07 Glow qualify too, subject to verification and available codes. The 2-hour wait is measured from the original Google Play purchase, not from this promotion or your submission. Refunded, cancelled, zero-price and previously fulfilled orders cannot receive a new code.

When does the 2-hour wait begin?

It begins at the purchase time recorded by Google Play, not when you first open or submit the form. A purchase that is already at least 2 hours old needs no additional waiting after verification.

What does the countdown mean?

HH:MM:SS shows the remaining wait before you can request another purchase check. Changing your device clock does not shorten the server-side waiting period. The countdown does not guarantee a code: the purchase must still qualify and codes must remain available.

Do I need to keep the page open?

No. You can close it and return later. Select the same purchased watch face, enter the same order number and submit again; we fetch the current purchase status and waiting time. There is no automatic email delivery — claim your code on this website.

Can I claim a second code for the same order?

No. One qualifying order gives you one free code. Repeated submissions or changing the selected free watch face do not issue another code.

How do I recover a code I already claimed?

Select the same purchased watch face and submit the same order in the same browser with this website’s data intact. The original code is returned after verification, even if you select a different free watch face. If you cleared website data or changed device, contact support instead of starting a new claim.

Which purchases are excluded?

Refunded, cancelled and zero-price purchases, including free giveaway redemptions, do not qualify. If we cannot reliably verify a purchase, we do not issue a code. CV08 Hybrid is not included in this BOGO offer.

When does the offer end?

The promotion ends on 31 January 2027. Codes must also be redeemed by that date; the exact cutoff time will match their confirmed validity. No promotion start timestamp has been announced. Do not wait until the last moment to redeem a code.

Contact arturteam1@gmail.com with the purchased watch face and a description of the problem. Never send passwords, recovery keys or Cloudflare verification tokens.
