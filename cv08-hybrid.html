CV08 Hybrid

Analog character. Digital clarity.

CV08 Hybrid combines classic analog hands with large digital time in a central panel. Four outer complications and four inner shortcuts let you make the face your own. Coming soon on Google Play.

Key features

  • Analog hands and large digital time
  • 4 editable outer complications
  • 4 editable inner shortcuts
  • Option to hide the hands and indexes
  • Color options for the background and central panel
  • Wake-up ring animation and a dedicated AOD

Two ways to read the time

Classic analog hands work alongside clear digital time in the center. Hide the hands and indexes for a simpler digital view.

4 complications and 4 shortcuts

Customize four outer complications and four inner shortcuts. Available data and apps depend on your watch, Wear OS version and compatibility between the selected source and field.

Customization

Choose a color option for the background and central panel, and decide whether to show the hands and indexes. The colors of the hands and indexes are fixed. The ring animates on wake-up; gallery images show still views.

Always-On Display

A dedicated AOD mode provides a simplified time display. Its behavior also depends on your watch and Wear OS settings.

4 outer complications and 4 inner shortcuts — 8 editable elements in total.

Can I hide the hands and indexes?

Yes. You can change their visibility, but their colors are fixed.
