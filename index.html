More complication options

Phone Battery Complication

Phone Battery Complication by amoledwatchfaces is required for selected additional complication features across the Clear Vision collection. The watch face itself works without this app, but data sources that depend on it will not be available.

Available data depends on the watch face and the selected slot type. Not every complication works in every slot.
