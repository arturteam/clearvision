Getting started

Installation guide

Follow these steps to install CV01 Essential and add it to your Wear OS smartwatch.

1

Open Google Play

Open the Google Play Store on your Android phone and make sure the phone is paired with your Wear OS watch.

2

Find CV01 Essential

Search for CV01 Essential or open the product page from the Clear Vision website after release.

3

Install on your watch

On the Google Play product page, select your smartwatch as the installation device and confirm the installation.

4

Add the watch face

Touch and hold the current watch face, swipe to the end of the list, select Add watch face, and choose CV01 Essential.

5

Customize complications

Touch and hold CV01 Essential, select Customize, and choose the information shown in the available complication areas.

6

Check permissions and data

If weather, heart rate, steps, or another complication does not appear, open the complication settings and select an available data provider.

Before installation

  • Your smartwatch must use Wear OS.
  • Your phone and watch should use the same Google account.
  • The watch should be connected to the internet.
  • Google Play availability may vary by device and region.

Need help?

If CV01 Essential does not appear on your watch after installation, restart the watch and check the watch-face list again.
