Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Clear Vision Watch Faces respects your privacy. This policy explains data practices for the Clear Vision Wear OS watch faces and this website.

Watch Face Applications

The Clear Vision watch face applications do not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information with the developer.

Permissions

The watch face may access system information provided by Wear OS, such as time, date, battery level, heart rate, weather, or step count, solely for displaying information on the watch face. No data is transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Website Analytics

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits, general device and browser information, approximate location, interactions with the website, and clicks leading to Google Play. Google may process technical information, including an IP address, to provide this service. Clear Vision does not receive raw IP addresses in Analytics reports.

Google Analytics is not loaded until you select “Accept analytics”. Advertising storage, advertising personalization, Google signals, and advertising data collection are disabled in this implementation.

Cookies and Local Storage

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visits. Your consent choice is stored locally in your browser for up to 180 days so the website can remember it.

Third-party Services

Website analytics data is processed by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. The watch face applications themselves do not use analytics or advertising services.

Data Sharing

Clear Vision does not sell personal information. Analytics data is processed by Google only after consent for the purposes described above.

Children's Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any updates will be published on this page.
