A brighter look. Clear Vision readability.

Animation and glow

CV07 can play a short glow animation when the watch wakes. Turn it off in customization if you prefer a calmer appearance. Gallery images show the watch face as still frames; they do not demonstrate the animation in motion.

Six fields for your information

Customize the three fields above the time and the three below it. Choose compatible data or shortcuts, such as battery, steps, weather, calendar, heart rate, activity or alarms. Availability depends on your watch, Wear OS, apps and the selected field type.

Choose how much you see

Change the colors of selected elements or hide the main graphic for a more minimal look. You can also hide the minimum and maximum temperature readings beside the clock. These options let you choose both the information and the visual detail you prefer.

Always-On Display

When the watch enters Always-On Display, the face uses a simplified view that keeps the time readable. Enable AOD in the watch settings; its availability and behavior also depend on your device and system settings.

How to customize

  1. Set CV07 Glow as your active watch face.
  2. Touch and hold the face, then select Customize.
  3. Choose data for the six complications and set the colors. Select whether to show the animation, main graphic and minimum/maximum temperatures.
  4. Confirm your selection, return to the watch face and check your data.

Option names may vary by watch and Wear OS version.

Extra data: Phone Battery Complication

The watch face works without Phone Battery Complication. This external app by amoledwatchfaces is required for selected additional data sources, such as phone battery. Install and configure it on both your Android phone and Wear OS watch. Available options depend on whether the selected field supports the data source.

Compatibility and complications

This watch face is designed for compatible Wear OS watches. Check device compatibility on Google Play before installing. Complication availability, appearance, text length and refresh behavior depend on your watch, Wear OS version and data provider. Not every complication works in every field. If a field remains empty, check the selected app’s permissions or choose another compatible source.

Questions about CV07 Glow

Can I edit all six fields?

Yes. CV07 Glow has six editable complications: three above the time and three below it.

Can I turn off the animation and main graphic?

Yes. Both options are available in watch face customization.

Can I hide the minimum and maximum temperatures?

Yes. Both readings can be switched off.

Can I display my phone battery?

Yes, in a compatible field, after installing and configuring Phone Battery Complication on both your phone and watch.

Does CV07 support AOD?

Yes. CV07 Glow includes a dedicated Always-On Display mode.
